# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Agentic-Skills a respectful, constructive and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, profession, geography or personal characteristics.

This Code of Conduct is based on the principles of the Contributor Covenant and applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other project spaces.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive project environment include:

- communicating with respect and professional courtesy;
- giving constructive, specific feedback;
- welcoming questions and first-time contributors;
- acknowledging different professional, country and institutional contexts;
- focusing criticism on ideas, evidence and implementation rather than people;
- correcting errors transparently;
- respecting privacy, confidentiality and safeguarding considerations;
- accepting reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, evidence and responsible use.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation or discriminatory language;
- personal attacks or deliberately demeaning comments;
- sexualised language or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation or deceptive claims about identity or experience;
- knowingly fabricated evidence, citations, project experience or performance claims;
- repeated disruption after a maintainer has asked for behaviour to stop;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Project standards

Because this repository addresses development-sector and responsible-AI workflows, participants should also avoid posting confidential organisational information, personal data, unpublished bid material, safeguarding-sensitive information or other restricted content in public repository spaces.

Disagreement about methodology, evidence, AI risk, localisation or technical design is welcome when expressed substantively and respectfully.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, comments, issues or other material that violates this Code of Conduct. Depending on severity and recurrence, maintainers may issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban a participant from project spaces under their control.

Reports should be made privately to the repository owner through a current private contact route listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile rather than by posting sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

All reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. Maintainers should respect the privacy and security of people who report conduct concerns.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual is publicly representing the project or its community.
