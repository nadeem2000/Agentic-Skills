# Ahmad Nadeem

## AI Integration Specialist for International Development

**Generative AI Workflows · Research & Evaluation · Proposals & Business Development · Knowledge Management · Responsible AI**

I help **development organisations, consulting firms, NGOs, INGOs, evaluation and research organisations, programme teams, and development professionals** integrate generative AI into real organisational workflows.

My focus is not generic prompting. I work on the practical layer between **AI capability and organisational practice** — turning recurring development-sector processes into structured, reusable and responsibly governed AI workflows.

### What I work on

- **AI for proposals and business development** — opportunity screening, RFP/TOR analysis, compliance, evidence retrieval, proposal development and QA.
- **AI for monitoring, evaluation and research** — evaluation design, evidence synthesis, qualitative-analysis support, triangulation and reporting workflows.
- **AI for donor intelligence and resource mobilisation** — opportunity discovery, eligibility review, strategic fit and capture support.
- **AI for organisational knowledge management** — controlled retrieval and reuse of institutional documents, lessons and technical resources.
- **Custom AI skills and assistants** — reusable domain and workflow capabilities for development-sector teams.
- **Responsible AI adoption** — AI readiness, use-case prioritisation, governance, privacy, evidence controls, human oversight and pilot design.
