# AI Integration Services for Development Organisations

**Ahmad Nadeem — AI Integration Specialist for International Development**

These services are designed for development consulting firms, NGOs and INGOs, evaluation and research organisations, donor-funded implementers, social-impact organisations, programme teams, business-development teams, MEL teams, and development professionals.

The emphasis is on **practical organisational workflows**, measurable use cases, human oversight and responsible adoption—not generic AI training detached from day-to-day work.

## 1. AI Readiness & Opportunity Assessment

### Client problem

Teams are experimenting with generative AI but lack a shared view of where it creates value, where it creates risk, and which use cases should be prioritised.

### Service

A structured review of organisational workflows, pain points, data sensitivity, staff practices, governance needs and realistic AI opportunities.

### Typical deliverables

- workflow and pain-point map;
- AI-use-case inventory;
- value / feasibility / risk prioritisation matrix;
- data and privacy considerations;
- human-oversight requirements;
- quick-win and higher-risk use-case separation;
- pilot recommendations;
- 90-day AI adoption roadmap.

### Engagement format

Focused diagnostic or short advisory assignment, typically suited to a small organisation, department, programme team or consulting practice.

### Intended outcome

A defensible shortlist of AI initiatives linked to organisational needs rather than technology trends.

## 2. AI Workflow Design & Implementation

### Client problem

Staff may use AI individually, but the organisation does not yet have repeatable, documented workflows with clear inputs, quality checks and accountability.

### Service

Design AI-assisted processes around real work such as proposal development, research, MEL, reporting, knowledge management, communications or administration.

### Typical deliverables

- current-state workflow map;
- redesigned AI-enabled workflow;
- task-specific skills or structured instructions;
- document and evidence controls;
- human-review and approval gates;
- templates and operating guidance;
- pilot implementation plan;
- monitoring indicators and refinement cycle.

### Engagement format

Single-workflow pilot, multi-workflow implementation, or advisory support to an internal transformation team.

### Intended outcome

Consistent organisational use of AI that can be taught, reviewed, improved and governed.

### Service

### Typical deliverables

- opportunity qualification framework;
- RFP/TOR analysis workflow;
- compliance and submission matrix;
- capture-note structure;
- capability and experience retrieval workflow;
- partner/expert profiling support;
- technical-proposal architecture;
- consistency and evidence QA;
- role definitions for AI and human reviewers;
- reusable proposal-team skills and templates.

### Engagement format

A pilot around one live or simulated opportunity, followed by adaptation into a reusable organisational process.

### Intended outcome

A more structured, evidence-aware and reusable bid-development process while retaining human ownership of strategy, claims, costing and submission decisions.

### Client problem

Evaluation and research teams manage large volumes of TORs, documents, evidence, qualitative material, frameworks and reporting requirements, but AI use can introduce serious risks if source traceability and professional judgment are weak.

### Service

Develop controlled AI-assisted workflows for research and evaluation tasks where automation supports—not replaces—qualified professional judgment.

### Typical deliverables

- TOR and assignment interpretation workflow;
- evaluation-question and methodology mapping support;
- evidence and document matrix design;
- literature/evidence synthesis workflow;
- research-tool drafting support;
- qualitative synthesis and coding-assistance protocol;
- triangulation and contradiction checks;
- report architecture and evidence QA;
- data-sensitivity and human-review controls.

### Engagement format

Methodological advisory, workflow pilot, team capacity development, or implementation support for an evaluation/research practice.

### Intended outcome

Faster and more structured handling of evidence-intensive tasks without treating model output as verified evidence or delegating consequential evaluation judgments to AI.

# Engagement principles

Across all services:

1. **Start from the problem, not the model.** AI is considered only where it can improve a real workflow or decision-support task.
2. **Separate assistance from authority.** AI may draft, structure, compare or synthesise; accountable human professionals retain consequential decisions.
3. **Treat model output as unverified until checked.** Evidence-intensive work requires source traceability and factual review.
4. **Minimise sensitive-data exposure.** Privacy, contractual obligations and safeguarding take precedence over convenience.
5. **Pilot before scaling.** New workflows should be tested against measurable criteria, failure modes and user feedback.
6. **Do not manufacture ROI.** Benefits are measured where possible; otherwise they are stated as potential benefits rather than proven results.
