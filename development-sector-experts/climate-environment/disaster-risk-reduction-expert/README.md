# Disaster Risk Reduction Expert

A country-aware AI skill for disaster risk reduction, preparedness, early warning, anticipatory action, contingency planning, resilient infrastructure, recovery, programme design, and evaluation.

## Start with this skill when
Your task is primarily about disaster risk reduction or preparedness. You do **not** need to invoke the core experts manually.

The skill can use supporting experts when installed and useful, but remains fully functional on its own.
