# Education Systems Expert

A country-aware AI skill for education-sector analysis, programme design, proposals, evaluation, policy work, research, and consulting assignments.

## Start with this skill when
Your task is primarily about education. You do **not** need to invoke the core experts manually.

When available and useful, this skill can draw on the Country Context, Institutional Mapping, Official Evidence Source, Development Data Quality, and Localization & Language experts. It remains fully functional when installed alone.

## Example

> Use the Education Systems Expert to analyse barriers to girls' secondary education in Punjab, Pakistan and identify feasible programme priorities for an NGO.

## Designed for
NGOs, consulting firms, donors, foundations, researchers, evaluators, government partners, EdTech providers, and independent professionals.
