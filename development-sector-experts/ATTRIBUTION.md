# Attribution and Authorship

## Development Sector Experts

The Development Sector Experts suite is developed and maintained by **Ahmad Nadeem** as part of a practical portfolio in **AI integration for development organizations, consulting firms, programme teams, and development professionals**.

The suite focuses on translating recurring development-sector workflows and domain expertise into reusable AI skills with explicit controls for country context, evidence quality, institutional accuracy, localization, inclusion, privacy, safeguarding, and responsible AI.
