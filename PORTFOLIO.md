# AI Integration Portfolio for International Development

## Ahmad Nadeem

**AI Integration Specialist for International Development**  
Generative AI Workflows · Research & Evaluation · Proposals & Business Development · Knowledge Management · Responsible AI

I work at the intersection of **international-development practice and applied generative AI**. My focus is not on isolated prompting or generic AI experimentation. It is on helping development organisations convert recurring professional work into **repeatable, quality-controlled, human-supervised AI workflows**.

The portfolio is designed for development consulting firms, NGOs and INGOs, evaluation and research organisations, donor-funded implementers, universities and think tanks, programme teams, business-development teams, MEL teams, and independent development professionals.

## What I help organisations improve

### Proposal, bid and business-development workflows

AI can support opportunity screening, RFP/TOR analysis, compliance mapping, partner and expert profiling, proposal architecture, evidence retrieval, draft development, consistency checks, and final QA. The objective is a stronger process with clearer human review—not automated submission.

### Research and evidence synthesis

I design workflows for structured literature review, source prioritisation, institutional mapping, document comparison, evidence extraction, synthesis, uncertainty handling, and citation discipline. The emphasis is on traceability and source quality.

### Monitoring, evaluation and learning

Potential workflows include TOR analysis, evaluation-framework development, question-to-method mapping, tool design, qualitative synthesis support, triangulation, evidence matrices, report structuring, and quality review. High-consequence judgments remain with qualified evaluators and technical experts.

### Donor intelligence and resource mobilisation

AI can help teams scan opportunities, structure donor intelligence, compare eligibility and thematic fit, maintain opportunity pipelines, prepare capture notes, and retrieve relevant institutional experience. Human validation remains essential for current donor rules, deadlines and compliance.

### Organisational knowledge management

I help organisations think through how institutional documents, prior proposals, evaluations, policies, technical resources and internal lessons can be made easier to find and reuse through controlled AI-assisted knowledge workflows.

### Responsible AI adoption

AI integration should start from organisational problems, not technology. My responsible-AI approach covers value, feasibility, privacy, evidence quality, human oversight, permissions, governance, change management, monitoring, vendor risk, and failure handling.

## Demonstration philosophy

A credible AI-integration portfolio should show more than instructions. Demonstrations in this repository are being developed around four questions:

1. What organisational problem exists today?
2. What should AI do—and what should it not do?
3. Where are evidence, data and human-review controls placed?
4. What output can a real team use?

No unverified productivity percentages or client outcomes are presented as facts. Where a benefit has not yet been measured, it is described as a **potential organisational benefit**.
